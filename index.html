Before you start

Your clock starts when your team validates the first code and stops at the last one. You earn a mudlark for each clue. Tie-break is time.

    The rules, plainly

    • Walk, do not run. Your time is measured, but crossing on red is a disqualification. Covent Garden is full of people.
    • Stay on the pavement. Do not block doorways, shop entrances or bus stops.
    • Every clue is in public space — plaques, statues, shopfronts, signs. Nothing is inside a venue.
    • If you use a cafe's table or toilet, buy something. No venue is paying for this.
    • No climbing, no touching, no trespassing. Everything is reachable at eye level from the street.
    • Photos are optional. If you post one, use #MudlarksLDN and we may repost it with credit. Message us to have it removed.
    One clue solved: one mudlark. Mudlarks are earned by playing and cannot be bought. No balance, no wallet, no transfers.
